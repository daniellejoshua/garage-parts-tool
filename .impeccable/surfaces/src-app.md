# GAP Marketplace Admin Application

## Scope

The shared shell and all existing Cars and Car Parts selection, listing, form, detail, media, loading, empty, and error surfaces. Visitor mode: Operate.

## Audience And Job

Internal staff repeatedly prepare accurate car and compatible-part seed listings for marketplace presentations. The primary task is moving confidently from module to vehicle context to listing creation and review, with no ambiguity about the active brand, model, or compatibility.

## Constraints

Preserve all current behavior, routes, data boundaries, field meanings, validation, and real repository assets. Avoid decorative analytics, invented export capabilities, consumer-marketplace styling, and novelty that slows CRUD work. Desktop efficiency leads, with complete mobile adaptation.

## Direction Contract

THESIS: A category-standard admin workspace executed with Linear-level precision. It refuses both the generic card-dashboard collage and decorative automotive theming; hierarchy comes from task relevance, alignment, and state.

OWN-WORLD: Restrained light surfaces, near-black ink, cool structural grays, and one decisive blue action color. Compact workhorse typography, tabular numerals, crisp one-pixel rules, quiet elevation, and consistent medium-radius controls make the system recognizable with content removed.

STORY: Staff always know which module, vehicle, record, and authoring stage they occupy. They choose Cars or Car Parts, narrow through the shared catalog, create or inspect listings, and verify state without decoding ornament or fake metrics.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A slim persistent application bar frames a concise overview. The main plane gives Cars and Car Parts equal, high-salience routes, each exposing its Brand to Model to Listings sequence and one direct Browse action. Supporting catalog truth stays visibly secondary; no KPI cards appear.

FORM: The chosen form is the category standard, intentionally selected over the roll, with Linear as the craft reference. Seed key 8c17906d. Navigation, tables, forms, dialogs, loading states, and responsive behavior use familiar admin patterns at unusually disciplined density and finish.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Memorable Moment

The brand-model-listing context remains visually continuous from selection through form and detail views, so a user can identify where a record belongs at a glance.
